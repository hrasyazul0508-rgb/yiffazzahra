A UNIVERSE THAT SHINES FROM WITHIN

Every fairy has a little light that is entirely her own.

WONDERVALE is a bright, ever-growing universe of fashionable fairies, transformative magic, and friendship that makes the impossible feel possible. When a fairy discovers the thing that makes her heart sing, her wings awaken—and a whole new adventure opens up.

At Starshine Academy, young fairies learn to trust their gifts, dream boldly, and look out for one another. Lessons might take place in the garden greenhouse, under a sky full of wishing stars, or halfway across a portal to another dimension. The best discoveries are the ones they make together.
